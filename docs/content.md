## About Us Page

Safe-Lynk is a healthcare recruitment and staffing agency supporting
organisations that need dependable healthcare professionals.

We understand that staffing in healthcare is different from ordinary
recruitment. The people being recruited are working with patients who may be
vulnerable and require compassion, patience, professionalism and the right
level of competence. That is why we take the recruitment process seriously.

We work with healthcare professionals to understand their experience and
career goals, while working closely with employers to understand exactly
what they are looking for. Our aim is to create long-term working
relationships, rather than simply filling vacancies.

### Why Safe-Lynk?
- **We understand healthcare** — Healthcare is not just another industry to
  us. We understand that the quality of staff supplied can have a direct
  impact on patient care and the day-to-day running of a service.
- **We listen** — We take time to understand what our clients actually need
  instead of simply sending CVs.
- **We know our candidates** — We want to understand the experience, skills
  and aspirations of the professionals we work with.
- **We take compliance seriously** — We understand the importance of
  appropriate pre-employment checks and maintaining accurate recruitment

  clearly and deal with enquiries promptly.
- **We build relationships** — Our goal is not to make a single placement
  and disappear. We want to develop lasting relationships with both
  healthcare organisations and professionals.

## Services / "What We Do" Page

### Healthcare Recruitment
We recruit and introduce suitable healthcare professionals for temporary,
permanent and, where applicable, flexible staffing opportunities.

Recruitment areas can include:
- Registered Nurses
- Mental Health Nurses
- Healthcare Assistants
- Support Workers
- Care Assistants
- Mental Health Support Workers
- Senior Healthcare and Support Staff
- Other healthcare professionals appropriate to the vacancies handled

The roles recruited for depend on client requirements and candidate
qualifications and experience.

### Temporary Healthcare Staffing
Healthcare services can experience unexpected staff shortages, increased
demand and changes in staffing requirements. We help organisations find
suitable temporary staff when they need additional support, responding
quickly while still maintaining appropriate recruitment and compliance
checks.

### Permanent Recruitment
Finding the right person for a permanent position is about more than
matching a CV to a job description. We take time to understand the role,
the organisation and the candidate before making an introduction — helping
employers find people who are not only suitably qualified but also a good
fit for the role.

### Our Recruitment Process (numbered steps)
1. **Application** — You submit your CV and relevant information about your
   experience and the type of work you are looking for.
2. **Initial Review** — We review your experience, qualifications and
   suitability for the type of roles you are interested in.
3. **Compliance Checks** — Before a candidate is put forward for a role, the
   appropriate checks are carried out: identity, right to work, professional
   registration, qualifications, employment history, references, criminal
   record (CRB) checks and occupational health requirements, as applicable.
   NHS Employers currently identifies six core employment check standards
   covering identity, criminal records, work health, professional
   registration and qualifications, right to work, and employment
   history/references.
4. **Matching** — Where there is a suitable vacancy, we match the
   candidate's skills and experience with the requirements of the
   organisation.
5. **Interview / Selection** — Where required, the candidate completes the
   client's interview or selection process.
6. **Placement** — Once all required checks and approvals are completed,
   the candidate can begin work in accordance with the agreed arrangements.

### Our Commitment to Safe Recruitment
Healthcare recruitment carries a responsibility to patients, healthcare
organisations and the professionals we place. We aim to maintain a thorough
and responsible recruitment process, verifying professional registration
and qualifications before healthcare professionals begin work, alongside
right-to-work, identity, employment-history and reference checks. Where
occupational health requirements apply, we work with the relevant parties
to ensure the appropriate process is followed.
